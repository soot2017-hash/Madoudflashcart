WORD: achieve
SOURCE: Cambridge Dictionary (dictionary-crawler)

HEADWORD: achieve
PART OF SPEECH: verb
PRONUNCIATION: [uk] /əˈtʃiːv/ | [us] /əˈtʃiːv/
SENSE — to succeed in finishing something or reaching an aim, especially after a lot of work or effort
example: The government's training policy, he claimed, was achieving its objectives.
example: She recently achieved her goal of completing a marathon.
example: She finally achieved her ambition to visit South America.
