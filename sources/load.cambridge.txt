SENSE — the amount of weight carried, especially by a vehicle, a structure such as a bridge, or an animal
example: The maximum load for this elevator is eight persons.
example: They delivered two loads of wood to the site.
example: One truck involved in the accident was carrying a heavy load of coal.
SENSE — a lot
example: I've got a load of work to get through before tomorrow.
example: There were a load of people there.
example: Have some more food - there's loads.
SENSE — the amount of electrical power that is supplied
SENSE — the amount of work to be done by a person
example: If we share the organization of the party, that will help spread the load.
example: I've got a heavy teaching load this term.
example: She has had to drop one course, so she’s taking a slightly lighter course load.
SENSE — a painful, difficult, or tiring situation to deal with
example: Living with a terminal diagnosis is a heavy load to carry.
example: I wish I could do something to lighten your load (= make your situation easier).
SENSE — an amount of clothing, etc. that is put in a washing machine to be washed, or that has been taken out of a washing machine after being washed
example: I always put a load in before I go to bed and hang it out to dry in the morning.
example: You sort the washing and put a load on, and when you go back to hang it out the clothes appear to have multiplied.
example: I've already done three loads of laundry this morning.
SENSE — to put a lot of things into a vehicle or machine
example: How long will it take to load this sand onto the lorry?
example: Let's load up the car and then we can go.
example: to load the dishwasher/washing machine
SENSE — to have too much to carry, or too much work to do
example: I was loaded down with shopping.
SENSE — to contain a lot of something
example: Most fast foods are loaded with fat.
SENSE — to fill or cover something with a lot of food
example: Guests loaded up their plates at the restaurant buffet.
example: On the day of the party the table was loaded with food.
example: a buttermilk biscuit loaded with bacon, egg and cheese
SENSE — to put film in a camera or bullets in a gun
example: Do not load the film (= put it into the camera) in bright light.
SENSE — to put information or a program onto a computer
example: You need to load this program onto your computer.
example: The software is easy to load.
SENSE — If a web page or an application loads or is loaded, it appears on a screen
example: My browser wouldn't load the page.
example: Some of the apps on my smart TV are quite slow to load.
example: What happens if the user clicks on a link before the web page has finished loading?
SENSE — in baseball, to have runners at each of the bases (= positions that a player must reach to score a point)
example: The Tigers loaded the bases with one man out in the fourth inning.

example: The total maximum load is four hundred kilograms.
example: Heavy passenger loads put stress on the nation's aviation system.

SENSE — the amount of work to be done by a person, an organization, or a machine

example: heavy/light/increased load
SENSE — a charge that you pay when you buy or sell shares in an investment company
SENSE — an amount of money, usually a large amount, that a person, company, or country has to pay
example: A ruling by the European Union lightened the tax load on investors buying gold.
SENSE — to put goods onto a vehicle to transport them
example: A surge in trade has seen up to 90 vessels a week at the port waiting to load their cargoes.
example: Trucks were loading and unloading at the warehouse.
example: The tanker was loaded with four million gallons of diesel oil.
SENSE — to put a program or data onto a computer or website
example: The software is very easy to load.
example: The updates don't take long to load.
SENSE — to include charges in an investment, loan, etc.
example: The card is designed to attract customers with poor credit and comes loaded with lots of fees and a very low credit limit.
SENSE — used with other nouns to refer to a vehicle, large container, etc. that is completely full
